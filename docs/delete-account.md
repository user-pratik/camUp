# Account & Data Deletion for camUp

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

At **camUp**, we prioritize user privacy and data ownership. camUp does not operate proprietary backend databases or servers to store your personal information or video recordings. All data is stored either locally on your Android device or directly within your personal Google Drive account.

This page explains how you can manage, delete, or request the deletion of your data and revoke account access.

---

## 1. Deleting Data Stored Locally on Your Device

All temporary video files, recording metadata, and local preferences are stored on your device:

* **Within the App:** You can delete individual video recordings or discard unfinished sessions directly from the recordings list.
* **Via Android Settings:**
  1. Open your device's **Settings**.
  2. Navigate to **Apps** > **camUp**.
  3. Tap **Storage & cache** > **Clear Storage / Clear Data**.
  4. Alternatively, **uninstalling the app** immediately removes all local database records, cached segments, and local configuration.

---

## 2. Deleting Uploaded Videos from Google Drive

Video recordings uploaded by camUp reside entirely in your personal Google Drive account:

1. Open [Google Drive](https://drive.google.com) on the web or via the Google Drive mobile app.
2. Locate the **camUp** (or **CameraBack**) folder.
3. Select and delete the files or the entire folder.
4. To permanently delete the files, open the **Trash / Bin** in Google Drive and click **Empty trash**.

---

## 3. Disconnecting Google Account & Revoking Permissions

You can disconnect your Google Account and revoke camUp's access to your Google Drive at any time:

1. Visit your [Google Account Third-Party Apps & Services](https://myaccount.google.com/connections) page.
2. Select **camUp** from the list of applications.
3. Click **Delete all connections you have with camUp** (or **Remove Access**).
4. Confirm your selection. Once revoked, the app can no longer access or upload files to your Google Drive.

---

## 4. Requesting Data Deletion or Inquiries

Because camUp does not store any of your personal data or video files on external servers, deleting local files and Google Drive files removes all associated data.
