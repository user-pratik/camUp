# Privacy Policy for camUp

**Effective Date:** August 25, 2026

Pratik Anand ("we", "us", or "our") operates the camUp mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

## 1. Information Collection and Use

The App is designed to provide a secure video backup solution. To achieve this, we access the following information:

### a. Media and Hardware Access
* **Camera:** We use the camera to record video at your request.
* **Microphone:** We use the microphone to record audio for your videos.
* **Storage:** We store temporary video segments on your device before they are uploaded to your Google Drive.

### b. Google Account Information
* **OAuth Authorization:** We use Google Identity Services to allow you to sign in and authorize the App to access your Google Drive. We only request the `drive.file` scope, which allows the App to see, create, and delete **only the specific files** that you open or create with this App.
* **Email Address:** Your email address may be accessed during the authentication process to identify your account for Google Drive storage purposes. We do not store this on any external servers.

## 2. Data Storage and Transfer
* **No Backend Servers:** All video recordings and metadata are handled directly between your device and your own personal Google Drive account. We do not operate any backend servers and never have access to your video files.
* **Local Database:** Metadata about your recordings (timestamps, upload status) is stored locally on your device in a Room database.
* **Encryption:** All data transferred between the App and Google Drive is encrypted in transit via HTTPS.

## 3. Data Retention and Deletion
* **User Control:** You have full control over your data. You can delete recordings locally within the App or manually from the "camUp" folder in your Google Drive.
* **App Uninstallation:** Uninstalling the App will delete all locally stored metadata and temporary video segments. Files already uploaded to Google Drive will remain there until you manually delete them.
* For detailed steps on deleting your data or disconnecting your account, see our [Account & Data Deletion Instructions](delete-account.html).

## 4. Third-Party Services
* **Google Drive API:** The App uses Google Drive services for file storage. Use of these services is subject to the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
* **No Tracking/Analytics:** We do not use any third-party analytics, crash-reporting, or advertising SDKs.

## 5. Children's Privacy
Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children.
